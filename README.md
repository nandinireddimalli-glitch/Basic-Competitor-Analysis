# Competitor Analysis: Indian Ed-Tech (K-12 and Exam Prep)

Internship task for Veda Technology: *Perform Basic Competitor Analysis*.

## Objective
Learn how marketers evaluate competitors and identify opportunities, by comparing three Indian ed-tech brands on the same metrics.

## Competitors analysed
| Brand | Why it was chosen |
|---|---|
| **Physics Wallah** (pw.live) | Creator-led, affordable, YouTube-first; now publicly listed |
| **Unacademy** (unacademy.com) | Large exam-prep brand that has scaled back |
| **Vedantu** (vedantu.com) | Live-tutoring pioneer focused on K-12 |

All three serve JEE/NEET and school students, so the same metrics apply to each.

## Approach
1. Defined one metric list for every competitor: website, SEO, content, social media and marketing approach.
2. Collected figures from public third-party pages (Semrush, Ahrefs, Qoruz, VidIQ) and news coverage. Every figure is dated and linked in the **Sources** sheet.
3. Calculated engagement metrics with formulas (engagement = (avg likes + avg comments) / subscribers; views as a share of subscribers).
4. Wrote strengths and weaknesses for each brand, each backed by a figure.
5. Looked for gaps none of the three cover well, rather than copying what they do.

## Key findings
- **Physics Wallah dominates free content.** Its flagship YouTube channel has about 14.3M subscribers and 1.2M average views per video, roughly 10x the main channels of Unacademy (1.4M) and Vedantu (1.3M, JEE channel).
- **Rivals split their audience** across many exam-specific channels, which lowers views per video (Unacademy 4.2% of subscribers, Vedantu 0.9%, Physics Wallah 8.4%).
- **Search traffic is mostly branded** for all three. Physics Wallah additionally wins large spikes from exam-result keywords.
- **Vedantu has the highest bounce rate** (69.64% vs Unacademy's 58.53%, Apr 2025).
- **Instagram engagement is far higher than YouTube engagement** for Physics Wallah (about 4.5% vs 0.32%).

## Three marketing opportunities
1. **Event-driven content hubs** for results, cutoffs, syllabus and counselling, to capture non-branded search in peak exam weeks.
2. **Short-form video and community** (Reels/Shorts plus Telegram/WhatsApp groups), where engagement is highest.
3. **One strong flagship free-content brand** per segment, instead of fragmenting audiences across many channels.

## Limitations
- Traffic and SEO figures are published third-party snapshots from different tools and months, not same-day exports, so compare within a row rather than across tools. They can be refreshed in Similarweb or Ubersuggest.
- Unacademy and Vedantu operate many YouTube channels; the channel used for each is stated in the sheet.
- Engagement figures from Qoruz and VidIQ are third-party estimates.
- "Not found" means no reliable public figure was located, not that the value is zero.
